“	CALLING ALL COOKIES!	„

Crumble Dungeon is a Challenge Dungeon in Cookie Run: Crumble where all obtained Cookies battle the squishy Holy Golden Drop. The player can enter the Crumble Dungeon up to 3 times daily using Crumble Dungeon Keys. The Cookie squad has up to a minute to deal damage for score and compete with other players.

Enemies

The Holy Golden Drop squashes on the floor to knock back Cookies, strikes Cookies with lightning, and summons Golden Solder Drops.
