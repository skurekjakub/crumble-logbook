## EXP Dungeon (경험치 던전) full-auto decks around stage 700, from DC and YouTube

### Bottom line
- **The best full-auto deck for 700+ has one damage dealer and eleven sustain cookies.** The DC post "경던 ㄹㄹ오토덱" (dc:79492) cleared **724** at 3.96G on auto. dc:80715 cleared **746** with no input at all, with the projectile-speed pet.
  - **Cookies:** Brightseeker, Pomegranate, Lime, Milk (captain), Ion, Dark Choco / Cheesecake, Grapevine, Rockstar, Nameless Cake Hound, Herb, Moon Rabbit. All are Lv100.
  - **Pets:** Furball Pup (털뭉치 멍뭉이, the "탄속펫"), Majestic King Choco Drop, Icy Birdie.
  - **Welfare (복지):** 의료보험 (supports' max HP +5%) and 방어구 수리 특약 (DMG reduction +10%).
- **Retrying is part of the auto method.** Every auto source says to keep pressing retry (VART, 누리머 and ND러너 videos, dc:73577, dc:80701).
- **The boss gets easier with the 10-08 update.** The Librarian's base ATK goes down, the knockback AoE cooldown goes up, and the inner book range shrinks. This comes from the patch-note frame in yt:XqIsKR4mHes (t=72s) and Chateaw's "scheduled at 8th October update" (yt:RaZght8lZZ8). Some walls reported on 10-07 should soften after that.

### Consensus core
In almost every deck: **Brightseeker, Milk (captain), Pomegranate, Cheesecake, Ion, Herb, Nameless Cake Hound, Rockstar, Moon Rabbit**. Above about 650 the last three slots go to Lime, Grapevine, and either Dark Choco or Rye. Pets are **Icy Birdie** (ally DMG reduction 12%) and **King Choco Drop** (ally max HP +20%), plus one flex pet: Furball Pup, Holy Baby Drop or Hot Doggie. Welfare always includes **방어구 수리 특약**.

**What kills the boss.** The Librarian is Dark and weak to Light, and Brightseeker's drone lasers are the only real damage. The rest of the team amplifies them:
- Milk gives ATK and the 지속 synergy Brightseeker uses.
- Pomegranate gives skill amp and 탄속 synergy; Furball Pup adds 탄속 synergy +50% and ATK +10% on top.
- Cheesecake gives ATK; Lime gives crit rate.
- Moon Rabbit lowers the boss's ATK and crit rate; Dark Choco lowers its DEF.

These effects come from the game catalog in `research/001-guild-conquest-meta/evidence/12-glossary-src/cookieruncrumble_app_api_catalog_gameplay.json`.

**What keeps it alive.** Milk, Herb and Grapevine heal (Grapevine targets low-HP allies). Ion and Rockstar shield. Hound and Icy Birdie reduce damage, and King Choco Drop raises HP.

**Swaps if a cookie or pet is missing**
- King Choco Drop: Soda Dollop (73577, 누리머), Candy Shade Pouch (누리머), or Holy Baby Drop / Hot Doggie (ND러너).
- Furball Pup: Hot Doggie (79492), Octo Wasabi (80701) or Holy Baby Drop (78450).
- Dark Choco: Rye (VART, 누리머).
- Strawberry Crepe: Skating Queen (77754).
- Macaron: Lime (comment on RaZght8lZZ8).
- Brightseeker: nobody offered a replacement. Its star level matters (10★ vs 5★, comment on 79492).

### Disagreements
- **One DPS or more.** 79492, 80715 and 78450 run one dealer with Dark Choco or Skating Queen. VART and 누리머 add Rye. 75870 says more dealers clear better.
- **Second welfare perk.** 열정페이 (VART, 누리머, ND러너, 73577) or 의료보험 (79492, 80715, 78207, 서신우, 77041). 아포 (yt:O3WxZZHsKyY) says to switch to the HP perk if cookies die when entering at 48s.
- **Gear preset.** The guild-conquest (토벌, damage-reduction) preset is better per 79421, 79478, 82563 and the 80715 comments. The stage preset is better per 80425 and a 79492 commenter who was short on damage. 77041 and 78836 use the arena preset plus Rooty for accuracy.
- **Does auto hold at 720+?** A commenter on zx8hJ6_37w8 says 720 can't be done without manual control. 81485 says the auto deck "works for one day". 82340 is stuck at 746 with 4.61G, while 80715 cleared 746 on auto.

### Boss mechanics that matter for auto
- The boss opens with a wide knockback twice, and the second one ends around 48s (서신우, 77003, 아포). Walking in early gets the team hit repeatedly (80471).
- Rows of flying books do the damage. A book hit leaves Milk standing still (76824). Manual players park in the gap between book rows (80126, 79968).
- If Milk, Brightseeker or Pomegranate dies, the run is lost and you retry (77003, 79386, 80693). Auto only works because Milk and the shields absorb the opening, then Brightseeker gets two or more skill casts (ND러너).
- A low power ratio caps damage. At a 35% damage correction the auto deck stops working (81139).
- Knockback resistance is the missing counter. The new Chardonnay buffs it and Little Ghost gives 20% (77282, 81710). The 10-08 update also weakens the boss.

### Files
Everything is under `<scratchpad>/expdeck/dc-yt/`:
- `manifest.jsonl` has a line per file: file, url, captured_at, source_id, tool, sha256, and `derived_from` for frames.
- `evidence/captures.jsonl` is the capture tool's own ledger.
- `evidence/list-*.tsv` are the DC search listings.
- `evidence/posts/*.md` and `posts/img/` are the posts and their images.
- `evidence/dcvideo/` holds each post's page HTML and its embedded mp4.
- `evidence/dcframes/` and `evidence/ytframes/` hold the frames and contact sheets.
- `evidence/yt/` holds the YouTube search and watch digests; `evidence/ytvideo/` the downloads, which stay in scratch.

**Things the record will need to know**
- The scrapers write only under `research/`, because `researchDir` is fixed in `packages/capture/src/paths.ts`. I ran `runCli` through a scratch wrapper (`scratchpad/cap.mts`) pointed at the scratch folder. The repo tree is clean and nothing is committed.
- `dc fetch` misses DC's embedded movie-player videos. It keeps only the 200×200 sticker GIFs (dccon), so I fetched the mp4s with curl.
- `youtube download` failed with a 403 on 0CUv152HVrA. I pulled that video and the rest directly with yt-dlp using `--js-runtimes node`.
