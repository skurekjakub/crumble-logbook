# 002 — PvP meta: Arena and Rumble Arena (Cookie Run: Crumble)

Status: in progress (started 2026-09-27; curated 2026-09-27; refreshed 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-10)

## Question

As asked: the same research as record 001 did for Guild Conquest, now for PvP, covering both regular Arena (아레나) and the new Rumble Arena (럼블 아레나; officially 와글와글 아레나, 와레나). For each mode: the meta teams and their counter-teams, every cookie's level, star level and position, sugar runes, gear, pets, perks and synergies, and why each choice is made. The user has a whale account with every cookie built.

As a falsifiable statement: for each PvP mode there is a small documented set of top teams, and for each one the community sources name the lineup, formation, levels, stars, runes, gear, pets and the teams that beat it precisely enough that someone with every cookie could reproduce it and pick a counter.

## Verdict

**Arena: supported.** Since the 10-08 update the strongest deck is the Bari–Oven–Cherry Cola dive deck with Chardonnay in Macaron's slot (`arena-bari-oven-chardonnay`): the two crit buffs don't stack, hers lands faster and wider, and with her the chargers kill in the opening. Rye decks now lose to it from about Bari 5★, down from 6–7★ before; under Bari 5★ the same deck runs Rye for Cherry Cola. crumb.gg's meta page puts its group first at 23.2% of regular Arena on 10-10. Crit RES stays on the bottom-left; the haste count and the weapon's crit line are argued.

**Rumble Arena: supported for the lineup, partly for the build.** Season 2 kept Season 1's passives, so the charge core survived. Its top 100 run the old standard 12 with Chardonnay for Macaron (`rumble-chardonnay-12`): Chardonnay on 85 teams, Macaron on 5, and 29 of 52 fully revealed teams exactly that 12 (Rockstar for Herb on 14 more). Slots, stars and gear of the top defenses aren't visible.

## Reasoning

- **Chardonnay replaced Macaron.** Only one crit buff of the kind applies (dc:83118; 서신우 on nv:51751); hers scales with Skill AMP and reaches 120% at 10★ (`evidence/r2026-10-10/03-sites/data-patches.json`, dc:83255), and posters swap her into every Macaron slot (dc:83191; nv:51299 and its blog post). An 18M Rye deck with her beat the same deck with Macaron at 30M (dc:83147). Macaron fell from 93.1% to 48.4% of crumb.gg's Arena lineups and from 88 to 5 of the Rumble top 100.
- **Arena's order now.** "A high-star Bari-Oven deck is simply the strongest" (dc:85244); tier 1 in regular Arena too (dc:84857). A 30M deck with Bari 7★ beat a 42M one with Bari 10★ (dc:83199); a 29.89M Lv.117 team beat a 34.79M Rye defense on an 8-win run (dc:84728). Rye's plan of outlasting the opening fails against Chardonnay's crit flood (dc:83352). Where Rye stops winning is argued: 5★ (dc:84765, dc:83548), 4★ against a 10★ Chardonnay (dc:84760), 7★ in one reply (dc:84760).
- **Under Bari 5★, swap Cherry Cola for Rye.** 서신우's guide and his cafe reply (`evidence/r2026-10-10/04-naver-global/yt/subs-JuDMhwANm1M/s-00.jpg`, nv:51751); his Rye version won 32.82M against 34.51M, Rye top at 2.03M and Chardonnay third at 1.10M (`evidence/r2026-10-10/04-naver-global/yt/frames-JuDMhwANm1M/f-03m00.0s.jpg`).
- **Pomegranate's beam goes to Projectile Speed receivers first.** Chardonnay and Milk take it whatever their ATK (dc:83476, nv:51299); the third beam goes by ATK, so posters level Pinot down to give it to Bari or Oven (dc:84783).
- **Gear: crit RES matters more, haste is argued.** Crit past 100% rolls higher crit tiers, so crit RES still cuts damage (dc:83660, dc:83688); 서신우 calls a crit RES preset essential and runs six haste with it (`evidence/r2026-10-10/04-naver-global/yt/subs-JuDMhwANm1M/s-02.jpg`, `s-03.jpg`). Most Bari players run zero haste and Skill AMP + crit up top (dc:84847, dc:85284, dc:85139), but one wins more on six (dc:85207). The six-haste rows are current again, as disputed. The weapon's second line splits between crit rate (서신우; dc:83829) and crit DMG (dc:83575, dc:84340).
- **Season 2 kept the charge buff.** crumb.gg's Arena page lists Charge max HP +30% and team DR +30% for Season 2 (`evidence/r2026-10-10/03-sites/crumbgg_arena-page-rumble-tab.md`); the client data holds one passive list for both seasons' group (`evidence/r2026-10-07/03-sites/cookieruncrumble_app_rumble-arena.html`); a post rules the Madeleine deck out of Rumble for the HP and DR buffs (dc:83386). Bari + Oven sit on 77 of the top 100 (`evidence/r2026-10-10/08-extract/crumbgg-figures.txt`).
- **Hidden defenders, by tier.** Both modes hide 1 defender at Diamond, 2 at Master, 3 at Elite Master and 4 at Grand Master and Champion (`evidence/r2026-10-10/03-sites/crumbgg_arena-page*.md`), which explains the placeholders on high-ranked cards.
- **Attacker advantage persists.** Revenges cost no gems and hide the attacking deck (dc:82852); players still doubt perks apply on defense (dc:84480, dc:85226), and one quoted explanation says some skills aim by arena side (dc:82853). The new help text on Mercenary Guild effects (`evidence/r2026-10-10/03-sites/data-patches.json`) isn't captured. Cause untested.
- **Eternal Lv.95 set: skip.** A paid package of pieces fixed at Lv.95 with fixed lines, the lance carrying accuracy (`evidence/r2026-10-10/03-sites/crumbgg_equipment-*.md`); every reply says don't buy (dc:83361, dc:83525).
- **Ruling kept: "Rye beats Bari" is mostly Rumble,** and now less so there: a 25M Bari deck killed a 30M Rye deck at the start (dc:83352), and Rye is on 8 of the top 100.
- **Ruling kept: Rumble's "turtle vs charge" is one team** (dc:77491, dc:77636, dc:80927, dc:81227).
- **crumb.gg grids sort by attack range.** Board grids give membership, not slots (`evidence/r2026-10-07/03-sites/SYNTHESIS.md`, dc:81770); Chardonnay sits in Macaron's longest-range place.
- **Stars are readable.** Yellow stars count to 5 and each pink star adds one (dc:78977, dc:78543).
- **"Conquest arena" doesn't exist.** Guild Conquest (길드 토벌전) is PvE (record 001). The closest PvP mode is 부스러기 쟁탈전 (Crumb Clash), unreleased: a datamine describes base raids with 6 cookies plus bear troops, ELO and 29 tiers (dc:79879), and a coming-soon tab sits in the dungeon menu (dc:78556, nv:48555). Its tables were emptied in the latest data.
- **바궁 means Wind Archer in PvP threads**, while record 001's glossary lists it under Princess Bari. In PvP it sits in the pre-Bari ranged decks and is called the last-place TSSR; PvP posts call Princess Bari 바리, 바공 or 비리. Record 001 stays unedited, so a name resolver needs a mode-aware rule or the post's context. `curated/glossary.json` flags the clash.
- **Rumble Arena's other names:** TW 熱鬧開戰競技場; a JP video tags it わちゃわちゃアリーナ (`curated/glossary.json` sources).
- **Reading `formation_slots`.** Column 1 as the back line and column 6 as the front is inferred from attack ranges. Slots come only from owner formation screens: opponent cards look mirrored, and crumb.gg grids list cookies by attack range, not position.
- **Rumble usage bars (`curated/usage.json`).** A core's bar is an upper bound: it counts top-100 teams whose hidden slots could hold the missing members. The confirmed share counts only teams with every member revealed. The turtle 8 isn't a separate team (see the one-team ruling above); the standard-12 row's deck is `rumble-standard-12`.
