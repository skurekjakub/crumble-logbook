## The meta now

The top is unchanged. In one 3000+ defense log from 2026-10-04/05, 9 of the 11 3000+ attackers ran exactly the standard 12 (Macaron, Oven Wanderer, Pinot Noir, Cheesecake, Pomegranate, Bari, Milk as captain, Hound, Ion, Cherry Cola, Herb, Moon Rabbit), and every one of those whose pet row is visible ran Hot Doggie, King Choco Drop and Holy Baby Drop at 20 (dc:81227-3/4/5). **H**

- Between about 10th and 50th, roughly half the Bari–Oven decks swap Cherry Cola for Vampire (dc:80759, one observer, M). The one Vampire attacker in dc:81227-4 also dropped Herb for Doughnut King and ran Mango Toucan + Fluffy Cheese Cat. **H** for that card, **M** for the share.

A commenter adds Rye's edge is partly the shared 30% DR, so the matchup needs re-checking once the buff changes (dc:78990).
- **Bari's Rumble star floor:** 8★ (dc:82000) vs 5★ (record ruling) vs "5★ is food, only 10★ matters" (dc:77408).
- **Bari's future:** dead once the charge buff rotates (dc:78478, dc:79581, dc:80446) vs revived by any strong new charger (dc:78478, dc:78990 comments).
- **Shop reset:** none for Season 2 (dc:82217) vs Thursday in two weeks (dc:82260) vs monthly (dc:81405).
- **Where the blind starts:** 2,000 pts / Elite Master (dc:77636, dc:80927, dc:80059) vs about rank 10,000 (dc:81069).
- **Revenge payout:** 25-39 shown on revenge screens vs +3 reported (dc:77491, dc:77636, dc:80927 vs dc:80901).
