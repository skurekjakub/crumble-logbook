Bari & Cherry Cola Story Team

Start here for ordinary story stages. Keep Cherry Cola while testing Strawberry Crepe swaps: Ion adds a shield, while Herb adds healing. For Redberry, replace Bari and Cherry Cola with Tiger Lily and Cool Mint, then fight just below the southern stairs. The story boss preset guide covers all three teams, survival swaps, and boss positioning.

Story Knock-up Resistance Team

Use Candy Shade Pouch for knock-up resistance and extra HP; Panda Dumpling is the fallback if you lack it. This Wind Archer version already includes Ion and Herb. Against Cool Mint, try the seven or eleven o’clock corner before rebuilding the team. The story boss preset guide covers all three teams, survival swaps, and boss positioning.

Use Wind Archer when his investment raises useful team Power; Cherry Cola can take his slot. Bari and Herb stay in this preset. Princess Bari Story Team

Use Bari with Scorpion for extra boss poison damage. If allies keep dying, replace Strawberry Crepe or Scorpion with Herb. Start with useful offensive Rune rolls for story when Bari survives; this developed roster still needs enough Power for the stage. The Princess Bari build guide explains the complete lineup, Herb swaps, and separate Rune priorities for story and Arena.

Cherry Cola Auto Story Team

Use Cherry Cola with two Damage Reduction Rune lines and favor Skill Amp. Keep Milk as captain and ahead in ATK for the intended buffs. Rye is the comfortable auto choice; Melon Soda needs closer positioning. Swap Chargemellow for Icy Birdie or Crepe for Ion when survival fails. The Cherry Cola auto story guide covers the complete Rune plan, defensive Pet swaps, and boss positioning.

General Purpose Team

Scorpion and Brightseeker handle bosses; Rye, Melon Soda, and Strawberry Crepe clear waves. Pinot Noir keeps attacks and healing moving through knock-ups. Keep Chargemellow for Multi-strike damage; swap Crepe for Ion Cookie Robot if the opening hit keeps killing your team. For PvP adjustments, see the Arena healing reduction guide.

Gingercraven Team

Keep GingerBright alive long enough to apply ATK Down, then let Ion's shield protect the team. Icy Birdie reduces incoming damage; Grapevine adds healing instead of Pinot Noir's anti-lift support. Retry early deaths or missed Scorpion poison. Swap Devil for Dark Choco if Devil keeps dying.
