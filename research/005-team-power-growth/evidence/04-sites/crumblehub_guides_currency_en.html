CURRENCY PRIORITY

Currency Spending Guide

Recommended priorities and main sources for Crystals, Victory Medals, Guild Medals, and Mileage.

Last updated 2026.09.01CRUMBLE HUB GUIDE
PREMIUM CURRENCY

Crystals

Crystals are an important currency used to strengthen Cookies and Pets. Because the supply is limited, set your spending priorities around your current progression and Cookie roster instead of spending them immediately.

Main sourcesMissions, quests, Codex rewards, and more
  1. 01

    Raise a featured Cookie to 5 stars

    Pick-up Cookie Cutters are available in more limited quantities than regular Cookie Cutters. Consider spending Crystals during the pick-up period to reach 5 stars.

  2. 02

    Raise core Cookies to 5 stars

    If Stage progression has stopped, strengthen core PvE Cookies such as Scorpion Cookie, Milk Cookie, and Pomegranate Cookie. Saving your Crystals is also a good choice while Stages are progressing smoothly.

ARENA CURRENCY

Victory Medals

Main sourcesArena rewards
  1. 01

    Buy Chocosteel and Metal Syrup first

    Chocosteel is used for Plate Enhancement, while Metal Syrup prevents Plate Enhancement level loss at 10 stars or above. Both have limited sources, so buying them first is recommended.

  2. 02

    Sugar Runes, Stellar Points, and other growth resources

    Choose resources such as Sugar Runes and Stellar Points according to your current growth needs.

  3. 03

    Gacha currency and a random SSR Cookie box

    These purchases are optional. Consider them when you have plenty of Victory Medals.

GUILD CURRENCY

Guild Medals

Main sourcesGuild attendance
  1. 01

    Buy Tea Knight Cookie first

    Tea Knight Cookie cannot be obtained through the gacha, so buying Tea Knight Cookie first is recommended.

  2. 02

    Chocosteel and Metal Syrup

    Enhancing a Plate to +20 consumes a large amount of Chocosteel and Metal Syrup, so buying them is recommended.

MILEAGE CURRENCY

Mileage

Main sourcesChest rewards; granted when a 10-star Cookie appears again in the gacha
  1. 01

    Daily Dungeon Keys

    The recommended order is Dough Dungeon Keys, Coin Dungeon Keys, then EXP Dungeon Keys.
