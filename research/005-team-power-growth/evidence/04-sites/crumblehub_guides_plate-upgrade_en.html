PLATE ENHANCEMENT

Plate Enhancement Guide

Enhancement milestones and priorities for Plates with offensive stats.

Last updated 2026.09.23CRUMBLE HUB GUIDE
  1. 01

    Goals for +5, +10, +15, +20, and +25

    A Plate's sub-options are amplified every 5 enhancement levels. Instead of stopping one Plate between milestones, enhance the Plates you need in 5-level increments.

    +5

    Gain the first sub-option amplification with a relatively small material investment.

    +10

    Amplify the sub-options of a core Plate one more step.

    +15

    Focus your materials on Plates with strong main and sub-options.

    +20

    Continue enhancing core Plates with strong main- and sub-option combinations.

    +25

    The new maximum in 1.4.002. Focus your materials on your best Plates.

    Rather than enhancing every Plate evenly, progress through +5 → +10 → +15 → +20 → +25.

  2. 02

    Enhance Swords, Bows, and Staves first

    Start with Swords, Bows, and Staves that have offensive main or sub-options.

    Main options

    SwordATK Increase
    BowAccuracy Increase
    StaffCRIT Rate Increase
  3. 03

    Sub-option combination examples

    The following sub-options improve offensive performance.

    Possible sub-options

    • ATK Increase
    • Accuracy Increase
    • CRIT Rate Increase
    • CRIT DMG Increase
    • Skill AMP Increase

    Good combination examples

    • ATK Increase + CRIT Rate Increase
    • ATK Increase + CRIT DMG Increase
    • ATK Increase + Skill AMP Increase
    • CRIT Rate Increase + CRIT DMG Increase
    • Accuracy Increase + ATK Increase
    • Accuracy Increase + CRIT Rate Increase

    For stages where you lack Accuracy, prioritize a combination that includes Accuracy Increase. If your Accuracy is sufficient, prioritize ATK, CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, and Skill AMP.

  4. 04

    Plate enhancement order

    OrderPlateMain optionWhy enhance it
    1SwordATK IncreaseRaises ATK, the foundation of all attack damage.
    2BowAccuracy IncreaseHelps reduce missed attacks against enemies with high Evasion.
    3StaffCRIT Rate IncreaseRaises offensive performance by increasing how often critical hits occur.
    4Other PlatesVaries by PlateCheck their main and sub-options, then enhance Plates with more offensive stats first.

    The exact order among Swords, Bows, and Staves depends on the stat you currently lack. Enhance Bows first when Accuracy is lacking; if Accuracy is sufficient, prioritize Swords or Staves.
