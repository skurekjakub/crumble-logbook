Plating increases the main stats and sub-stats for a type of Gear. Each Plate's level is maintained even as Gear is changed.

  • Sword
  • Bow
  • Staff
  • Helmet
  • Armor
  • Shield
  • Necklace
  • Ring
  • Brooch
  • Artifact
  • Book
  • Food

Upgrading

Chocosteel is used to attempt Plating, with each Plate level requiring more Chocosteel and increasing the chances of negative outcomes.

  • Super increases the Plate's level by 2 and refunds some of the materials used.
  • Upgrade increases the Plate's level by 1.
  • No Change means the Plate's level remains the same.
  • Downgrade knocks the Plate's level down by 1 and only occurs above level 10.
  • Break knocks the Plate's level down to level 10 and only occurs above level 15.

Metal Syrup can be used to reduce the chance of a Downgrade or Break.

  • Block Downgrade prevents a Plate Downgrade.
  • Prevent Breaks halves the chance of a Plate Break.
Chocosteel
The main material used during Plating Reinforcement. This glorious chocosteel has been folded over a thousand times, and is vastly superior to any other metal on Earthbread.
Metal Syrup
An additional material used as a safety option during Plating. The sticky, adhesive syrup gives an added layer of protection to your Plates' levels.
