The Oven in Cookie Run: Crumble is used to bake Gear, which can be equipped or sold for 100 Sugar Flames each. Sugar Flames stoke the Oven to increase the Oven Level, raising the chances of baking higher rarity gear. Additionally, each Oven Level adds a new type of Gear, with 12 types of Gear in total.

Sugar Flame
Used to level up the Oven. Every last drop of the Sugar Gnome's sugar sweat and tears went into this. Don't take it for granted.


Ingredients

  • 1 Auto Bake
  • 2 Stoke Oven Gauge
  • 3 Probabilities
  • 4 Changelog

Auto Bake

The Auto Bake function automatically bakes Gear based on a minimum Gear Rarity and selected Sub Stats. If the Oven holds 15+ Gear, the Auto Bake Results menu can sell all Gear or Organize Gear—equip higher power Gear and sell the rest. Once the Oven holds 30+ Gear, the Auto Bake process stops. Auto Bake can open more items for every 5 Oven Levels reached. The available Auto Bake counts are as follows:

  • 1 at Oven Lv.1
  • 2 at Oven Lv.5
  • 5 at Oven Lv.10
  • 10 at Oven Lv.15
  • 20 at Oven Lv.20
  • 30 at Oven Lv.25
  • 50 at Oven Lv.30
