Crumble

Collections in Cookie Run: Crumble are combinations of Cookies and Pets such that every Collection involves every Cookie and Pet from launch exactly once. Each collection has a theme and story. Collection Milestones reward 100 Crystals and provide Collection Effects: ATK, DEF, or HP for all Cookies of a specific Role or Element.
