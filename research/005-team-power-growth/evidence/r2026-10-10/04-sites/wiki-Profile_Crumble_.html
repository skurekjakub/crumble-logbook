Profile is a menu in Cookie Run: Crumble for a customizing a profile, accessing Crumble Level, and viewing player stats. On the header, a player can change their username, title, Icon, or Frame, view and copy their MID, and view their Crumble Level progress.

Crumble Level

Crumble Level is the accumulative levels from Main Stages and Auto Hunt.
