The Mercenary Guild is a mechanic for managing and upgrading the Lineup. The Mercenary Guild is introduced after the player names it.

Ingredients

  • 1 Mercenary Guild Level
  • 2 Blast Mode
  • 3 The Leader
  • 4 Perks

Blast Mode

Defeating a certain amount of enemies triggers Blast Mode. During Blast Mode, the Lineup rapidly run around dealing massive damage to enemies. During Giant Blast Mode, Cookies in the Lineup grow larger and emit blue flames as well.

The Leader

Upon reaching Mercenary Guild Lv.1, the Cookie with the highest ATK is automatically appointed as the Leader. The player can appoint a different leader at any time.

The Leader bears the Mercenary Guild flag and leads other Cookies in the Lineup. The Leader may gain special effects from the Mercenary Guild. These effects only apply while the Leader is in the battle.

Perks

The player can select Perks for benefits to their Lineup. The first Perk slot is unlocked at Mercenary Guild Lv.1. The second Perk slot is unlocked at Mercenary Guild Lv.4. Unlocking a Perk requires both a minimum Mercenary Guild Level and completing its mission.
