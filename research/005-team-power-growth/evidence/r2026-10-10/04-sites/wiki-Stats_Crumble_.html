ATK

ATK determines the amount of damage dealt to enemies and in some cases, the amount of healing to allies.

DEF

DEF subtracts damage received by a flat number.

HP

HP determines the amount of damage an ally can receive until they faint at 0 HP.

CRIT%

CRIT% determines the chance of landing a critical hit. When CRIT% is greater than 100%, the CRIT DMG bonus can apply multiple times.

CRIT RES

CRIT RES reduces the chance of receiving a critical hit.

CRIT DMG

CRIT DMG multiplies the damage dealt by critical hits. When CRIT% is greater than 100%, the CRIT DMG bonus can apply multiple times.

Accuracy & Evasion

The difference between Accuracy and Evasion determines the chance of attacks hitting.

Focus & Resistance

The difference between Focus and Resistance affects the chance of debuffs applying.

DMG Reduction

DMG Reduction reduces damage received by a percentage.

Movement Speed

Movement Speed determines movement speed during battle, but not in search of enemies between battles.

Skill AMP

Skill Amp multiplies skills' damage, healing, and buffs' effects.

Skill Haste

Skill Haste reduces skill cooldown.

Boss DMG

Boss DMG multiplies damage dealt to bosses.

Elemental DMG

Elemental DMG increases damage dealt from an elemental advantage.

!See also: Element (Crumble)

Status Effect Stacking

When repeated or overlapping status effects are active, only the strongest is applied.

Lift RES

Lift causes Cookis or enemies to be airborne for a certain amount of time. If Lifted for more than 0.4 seconds, all active Skills are cancelled. Lift RES reduces the time lifted equal to its value.

Push RES

Push knocks back Cookies or enemies with a certain amount of force. Being Pushed while Lifted causes Cookies/Enemies to fly even farther. Push RES reduces the force taken when being Pushed back.

Changelog

  • August 13, 2026 (v1.1)
    • Accuracy, Evasion, Focus, Resistance, and Skill Haste stats are now displayed to the hundredth decimal.
    • Summon-related stats are now displayed with the multiplier already applied.
    • Issues causing Skill AMP not to be applied to aspects of certain Cookies’ Skills have been fixed.
      • Milk Cookie’s Crunchy Strong Pediatrician: ATK Increase
      • Rockstar Cookie: Shield
      • Ion Cookie Robot: Shield
    • Issues causing Skill AMP to not affect Summons have been fixed.
      • A higher Skill AMP stat now increases Summons’ ATK, DEF, and HP accordingly.
