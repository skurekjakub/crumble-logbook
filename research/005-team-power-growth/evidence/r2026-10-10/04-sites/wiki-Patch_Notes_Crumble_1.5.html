KEY UPDATES

NEW COOKIE — (SSR) CHARDONNAY COOKIE

This priest from the D'Vine Abbey can be both a blessing to her allies and a poison to her foes. Now, she's ready to stand on the side of your Mercenary squad!

  • Chardonnay Cookie is an SSR Grass Support Cookie who creates a Sanctuary of Verdance.
  • Her barrier of verdance grants allies within its linear path a CRIT% and Push RES buff, increases Volley Synergy, and deals damage to enemies.

NEW MAIN STAGES & NEW REGION

Continue your adventure and enter the D'Vine Abbey in new Stages!

  • Main Stages expanded to 364-30.

TEAM BUILDING CONTENT EXPANSION

  • The Gnome Laboratory is prepared for further research, up to Research 130-2 and Special Research 22.
  • Cookies' max. level increased to Lv.120.

RUMBLE ARENA SEASON 2

  • A new season begins in the Rumble Arena, where Mercenaries can face off against other Mercenaries from any server!

COLLECTION, COOKIETALK, & ADVENTURE NOTE UPDATES

  • New Collection entries featuring new groups of Cookies and Pets!
    • 4 Cookie Collections
    • 1 Pet Collection
  • Check out the Cookies' backstories and conversations with the new CookieTalk and Adventure Note updates!

EVENTS

NEW COOKIE RELEASE EVENT

  • Chardonnay Cookie Pick-up Event: Complete missions to get resources that will help you get Chardonnay Cookie from the Pick-up Gacha!
  • Unused Pick-up Points are reset when the event ends.

KING CHOCO DROP'S ROULETTE

  • Complete event missions to collect Roulette Tickets to spit the roulette and get your dough on sweet rewards!
  • Unused Roulette Tickets will be exchanged for a set amount of Coins when the event ends.

NEW CHECK-IN EVENT

  • A new 7-day check-in has begun!
  • Check-in for 7 days to earn special rewards, including a chance to meet Milk Cookie's Crunchy Strong Pediatrician!

IMPROVEMENTS & CHANGES

DAILY DUNGEON BALANCING

  • EXP Dungeon: Nightmare-ridden Librarian's basic ATK has been reduced, the cooldown for the Push attack has been increased, and the range of the inner ring of books has been reduced.
  • Dough Dungeon: Tapioca Froggies and Dumpling Cake Hounds have had their ATK reduced.
  • Innovite Dungeon: Dangerous Agar Jellies have had their HP reduced, and spawn locations adjusted and spawn speed increased.

MAIN STAGE BOSS BATTLE BALANCING

  • Basic enemies appearing during Boss battles have had their ATK reduced to be less than that of the Boss.

BALANCING COOKIE EXP REQUIREMENTS

GNOME LABORATORY CHANGES

  • The max. amount of time any Gnome Laboratory research can take is being reduced to 12 hours.
  • Players who have previously completed Research that took over 12 hours will have the excess time returned to them in 1-Min Speed-ups.

DIMENSIONAL RIFT REWARD & EXCHANGE IMPROVEMENTS

  • Metal Syrup, SSR Select Boxes, and 1-Min Speed-ups are added to the stage completion rewards.
  • More Chocosteel and Metal Syrup are now available in the Dimensional Rift Exchange.

MILEAGE EXCHANGE ADDITIONS

  • Chocosteel and Metal Syrup added to the Mileage Exchange.

PET SHELTER QoL IMPROVEMENTS

  • Collect all gifts from your pets in one go instead of having to tap on every bubble!

ARENA INFORMATION POP-UP UPDATED

  • The Arena information pop-up now includes information on how Mercenary Guild Effects are applied.

BUG FIXES & OTHER INFORMATION

BUG FIXES

  • Fixed issue causing Floating Gift Boxes to not appear in the Dimensional Rift.
  • Fixed issue where the Auto Hunt rewards in the Dimensional Rift were not shown in Auto Hunt information before entering the Dimensional Rift.
  • Fixed issue where the Gear Organization pop-up caused Auto Bake to stop.
    • The Organization pop-up no longer appears while Auto Bake is running. The pop-up appears when tapping on your newly baked Gear.
    • Auto Bake pauses while the Organization pop-up is open.

ISSUES ADDRESSED IN THE OCTOBER 2 UPDATE

  • Fixed issue where Boss Summons in the Dimensional Rift were not being counted as intended.
  • Fixed issue where the Boss Summon missions in the Crumble Pass and Rumble Special Pass were not being counted as intended.
