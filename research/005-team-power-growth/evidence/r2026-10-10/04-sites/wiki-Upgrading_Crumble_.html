Cookie Levels

Cookies can be leveled with Cookie EXP. Higher Crumble Levels increase the maximum Level of Cookies (up to 100). The Reset Level button resets a Cookie's Level to 1 and returns all the Cookie EXP used for leveling up.

Promotion

Cookie Promotion

A Cookie can be promoted after the player acquires enough copies of the Cookie (up to 10 times). Promoting a Cookie increases the Cookie's stats (ATK, DEF, CRIT%, CRIT DMG, Accuracy, Evasion, Focus, and Resistance). At 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9 stars, a Cookie's skill is enhanced.

Pet Promotion

A Pet can be promoted after the player acquires enough copies of the Pet (up to 20 times). Promoting a Pet increases the standard Pet Effect. At 5, 10, 15, and 20 stars, the Companion Effect increases.
