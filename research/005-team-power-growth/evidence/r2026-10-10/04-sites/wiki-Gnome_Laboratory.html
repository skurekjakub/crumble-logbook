Build is a section in Cookie Run: Crumble for upgrading Cookies. Upgrades apply to Main Stages, Crumble Dungeon, Daily Dungeons, and Plaque Tower.

Ingredients

  • 1 Resolve
  • 2 Fame
  • 3 Gnome Laboratory
  • 4 Stellar Links
  • 5 Changelog

Resolve

Resolve boosts the ATK, DEF, and HP of Cookies in the Lineup. The Resolve Level of individual stats are leveled in x1, x10, or x100 increments (set by the player) using Coins until a Resolve Stage is complete.

Fame

Fame enhances Boss DMG, Elemental DMG, and CRIT DMG. Leveling up Fame usually increases one of the three stats—at specific levels, all stats—and costs Fame Medals earned from Arena battles.

Gnome Laboratory

!Not to be confused with the Sugar Gnome Laboratory.

The Gnome Laboratory boosts combat stats with Basic Research and enhances mechanics with Special Research. Completing earlier stages of Research unlocks higher stages in the Research tree. Some Basic Research have multiple stages for better boosts.

  • Basic Research costs Basic Innovite and time.
  • Special Research costs Special Innovite and time.

Only one Research project can be active at a time, but they can be sped up with ads or speed-ups.

Research Effect
Basic Research 1-1 ATK AMP
Basic Research 2-1 DEF AMP
Basic Research 2-2 HP AMP
Basic Research 3-1 CRIT%
Special Research 1 +1 hour Maximum Auto Hunt duration
