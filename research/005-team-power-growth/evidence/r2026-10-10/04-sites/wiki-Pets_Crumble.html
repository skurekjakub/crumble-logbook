This List of Pets details all of the equipable pets in Cookie Run: Crumble. Pets provide permanent stat boosts once obtained, and up to 3 pets can accompany the party to provide extra stat boosts, with some SSR-Rank pets providing Synergy bonuses too. Pets will also regularly provide various gifts to the player at the Pet Shelter.

There are currently 55 Pets. At launch, the game featured 54 Pets.
