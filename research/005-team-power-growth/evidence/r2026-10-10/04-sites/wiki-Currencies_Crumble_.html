Currencies are resources in Cookie Run: Crumble exchanged for various goods, actions, and upgrades. Currencies can be obtained through naturally playing the game or through microtransactions in the Shop.

Coins and Crystal can almost always be seen. More currencies are visible in either the currency drop down menu or their respective game modes or mechanics. Some currencies can be accessed in the inventory.

Standard

Crystals

Crystals
A glorious and elusive Currency found throughout the CookieRun games. Crumble is no different.

Crystals are the premium currency used for Gacha or refreshing opponents in Arena. Crystals are primarily obtained from quests, missions, events, and microtransactions.

Coins

Coins
Used to level up Resolve. Look closely, and King Choco Drop might just wink at you.

Coins are a C rarity currency used to level up Resolve. Coins are primarily obtained from battles, auto hunt, quests, missions, events, and microtransactions.

Crumble EXP

Crumble EXP
Experience points needed to increase your Crumble Level. Don't worry too much—you'll get these throughout your adventures!

Crumble EXP are a C rarity currency that increases the player's Crumble Level. Crumble EXP is primarily obtained from battles and auto hunt.

Cookie EXP

Cookie EXP
Used to level up Cookies. Hey, who knew learning was as easy as just snacking on these?


Cookie EXP is used to level up Cookies, with the amount used increased with each level. It is obtained through normal gameplay, quests, various game modes or from Auto Hunt rewards. Players can also reset Cookies to get all the used Cookie EXP back.

Lucky Dough

Lucky Dough
Used to bake Fortune Cookies that contain Gear in the Oven. It's always exciting to see what your Fortune might hold!

Lucky Dough is used to obtain Gear from the Oven, consuming one per Gear. It is obtained from quests or event missions.

Sugar Flame

Sugar Flame
Used to level up the Oven. Every last drop of the Sugar Gnome's sugar sweat and tears went into this. Don't take it for granted.

Sugar Flame is used to level up the Oven. It is mainly obtained from selling Gear, though it is also rarely obtained from quests or Passes.

Gacha

Cookie Cutters

Premium Cookie Cutter
A Cookie Cutter used in the Premium Cookie Gacha. May the gacha gods be on your side!
Pick-up Cookie Cutter
A Cookie Cutter used in the Cookie Gacha. May the gacha gods be on your side!

Cookies cost 200 Crystals or 1 Premium Cookie Cutter per draw and allow tenfold draws (2,000 Crystals or 10 Premium Cookie Cutters) or thirtyfold draws (6,000 Crystals or 30 Premium Cookie Cutters). Aside from the permanent banner, there are also Pick-Up banners that present "Rate Up!"s for specific Cookies, often reserved for newly-released Cookies while costing the same as the standard Cookie Gacha. However, featured banners will use Pick-up Premium Cookie Cutters instead.

Pet Tickets

Premium Pet Ticket
A Pet Ticket used in the Premium Pet Gacha. You can never have enough adorable Pets, huh?
Pick-up Cookie Cutter
A Pet Ticket used in the Pet Gacha. You can never have enough adorable Pets, huh?

Similar to Cookies, Pets cost 100 Crystals or 1 Premium Pet Ticket per draw and also allow tenfold draws (1,000 Crystals or 10 Premium Pet Tickets) or thirtyfold draws (3,000 Crystals or 30 Premium Pet Tickets). Aside from the permanent banner, there are also Pick-Up banners that present "Rate Up!"s for specific Pets, often reserved for newly-released Pets while costing the same as the standard Pet Gacha. However, featured banners will use Pick-up Premium Pet Tickets instead.

Upgrades

Innovite

Basic Innovite
A currency used for Basic Research at the Gnome Laboratory. What, you didn't think research was free, did you?
Special Innovite
A currency used for Special Research at the Gnome Laboratory. What, you didn't think research was free, did you? Especially special research!

Innovite is used for researching upgrades at the Gnome Laboratory. Most upgrades use Basic Innovite, but specific ones use Special Innovite instead.

1-min Speed-up

1-min Speed-up
Reduces wait time by 1 minute. The physics behind how time shrinks instantly have been redacted.

Speed-ups are used to reduce upgrade time at the Mercenary Guild and Gnome Laboratory.

Rune Crystals

Rune Crystals
A crystal used for Sugar Rune Crafting. The true power of Sugar Runes originates from this Rune Crystal.


Fame Medal

Fame Medal
Used to increase your Fame. They say it can only be obtained in the Arena. Put your bravery to the test!

Fame Medals are used to level up Fame. Fame Medals are mainly obtained from the Arena.

Stellar Points

Stellar Point
Points used to reset Stellar Links. Gather enough, and you might have any wish come true!


Metal Syrup

Metal Syrup
An additional material used as a safety option during Plating. The sticky, adhesive syrup gives an added layer of protection to your Plates' levels.


Chocosteel

Chocosteel
The main material used during Plating Reinforcement. This glorious chocosteel has been folded over a thousand times, and is vastly superior to any other metal on Earthbread.


Exchanges

Victory Medals

Victory Medals
Can be exchanged for various goods in the Arena Exchange. Put your bravery to the test!


Guild Medal

Guild Medal
These can be spent in the Guild Exchange. Obtained via various Guild activities. Everything is better with friends, even rewards!


Mileage Points

Mileage Points
Used in the Mileage Exchange. Save them up and use them where it matters.


Mileage Points are earned either from drawing Cookies & Pets from the Gacha or from Auto Hunt rewards. Each draw will give a certain amount of points which can be redeemed at the Mileage Exchange for various rare upgrade items, tickets or instant Auto Hunt reward items. Drawing duplicates of maxed-out Cookies & Pets grants additional Mileage Points, with the amount of points received depending on the rarity of the Cookie or Pet.
