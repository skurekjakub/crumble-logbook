!Not to be confused with Cookie Gear (Toppings and Beascuits) or MyCookie Gear from Cookie Run: Kingdom.

Gear in Cookie Run: Crumble boosts the stats of Cookies in the Lineup. Gear is obtained from the Oven and enhanced with Plating.

  • There are 12 types of gear: Sword, Bow, Staff, Helmet, Armor, Shield, Necklace, Artifact, Ring, Book, Brooch, and Food. Each type of gear has its own Main Stat and possible Sub Stat. Types of gear are unlocked from leveling the Oven.
  • There are 10 rarities of gear: Normal, Advanced, Excellent, Magic, Epic, Special, Super Epic, Unique, Legendary, and Eternal. The chance of obtaining higher rarity Gear depends on The Oven's Oven Level.
