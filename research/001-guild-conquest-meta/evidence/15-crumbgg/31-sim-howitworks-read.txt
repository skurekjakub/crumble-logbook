# Simulator

How does this work?

## How this works

- Pick 12 cookies, 3 pets and 2 mercenary cards, then press Simulate.

- The website plays the whole Guild Conquest fight a few hundred times with the game's own numbers and shows how the lineup did.

- Every lineup runs on the same fixed account, so the results compare lineups. They don't predict your own score.

- Results are a value from 0 to 100, not raw damage: "typical" is the median fight, "best" the highest. The scale is stretched at the top, so losing a fifth of the damage costs about 5 points.

- The best fight of the run can be replayed.

- You need an account to run it. Compare, What-if, Gear and the Swap finder are limited to guild.

## Before you run it

- Cookie movement can't be modelled exactly, so how often certain things happen in a fight can be off.

- Skill Haste shifts when every skill goes off, so with different haste on your cookies the same fight can play out differently in your game.

- Treat the results as a guide, not as fact.

- Short runs are less reliable: with few fights the rare perfect run may not show up at all.
