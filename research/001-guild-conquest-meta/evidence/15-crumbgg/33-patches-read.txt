Sep 23, 20261.4.002

## Princess Bari, Rumble Arena and Dimensional Rift

Princess Bari

TSSR · Fire · Charge

Soul Guidance: charges at high-ATK enemies, then guided souls around her and her allies give DMG Down, Push RES and Lift RES and hit nearby enemies.

Every 5.5 s: 16 slashes of 79.7% ATK. Souls on her and 2 Charge allies deal 141% ATK every 0.66 s for 3.5 s (30% less for each repeat hit on the same enemy). DMG Down 40%, Lift RES and Push RES +100% for 3.5 s. Skill Prep 50%.

Moon Rabbit

SSR · Grass · Tank

Rams into enemies, then turns into a Giant Rice Cake Bunny to deal more damage and lower their ATK and CRIT%.

Every 7 s: 3 smashes of 38.8% ATK around her. 60% chance to lower ATK by 50% and CRIT% by 130% for 5 s (one roll for both). Skill Prep 50%.

Candy Shade Pouch

SSR · Pet

Ally Lift RES +30%, Max HP +12.5%.

Same effect group as Panda Dumpling's Lift RES: they don't stack, the higher one counts.

Continuum Cog

SSR · Pet

Ally Boss DMG +150%, only in the Dimensional Rift. From the Dimensional Rift exchange.

+50% at the lowest grade, +150% at the highest. No effect in Guild Conquest.

### Cookie & pet changes

- ▲ BuffSkill Prep0% → 50%

- ▲ BuffSkill Prep0% → 25%

### New

- Rumble Arena: fight Mercenaries from other servers. Irregular seasons, each with its own passive effects. Rumble Medals go to the Rumble Arena Exchange. New Rumble Special Pass.

- Dimensional Rift: opens after the last main stage. Boss-only stages; raise your Dimensional Energy level for stats. Season rewards: profile frames and Dimension Fragments for the exchange.open

- Crumble Patches: collectibles earned from rewards and achievements, shown on your profile.

### Balance

- New stat Skill Prep: shortens the wait for a cookie's first skill. Cookies that already cast at the start show 100%.

- Main stages 169-1 to 328-30 made easier; the bosses of 169-1 to 248-30 too (Cool Mint Cookie, Rowdy Biker, GingerCraven, Cream Eagle, Tainted Ent, Redberry Assassin, Choco Werehound Princess).

- Recommended power, enemy ATK and HP on those stages: from almost the same at 169-1 to 30% lower at 248-30 and 36% lower at the end. Stage 328-30 power:15.61B → 10.00B

- Guild Conquest tier requirements lowered: Chocolate 1 100M → 15M, Bronze 2 150M → 30M, Bronze 1 200M → 50M, Silver 3 300M → 100M, Silver 2 450M → 200M, Silver 1 650M → 300M, Gold 3 1G → 600M, Gold 2 1.5G → 1.1G.

- Guild Conquest group rewards (Guild Medals): 2nd 1,400 → 1,500, 3rd 1,000 → 1,200, 4th 600 → 1,000, unranked 200 → 900. From season 6 (Sep 24).

### Expansions

- Daily Dungeons up to stage 770.

- Gnome Laboratory up to research 110-2 and special research 19.

- Plating up to level 25.

### Events

- Soothing Flame: collect Wisps of Guidance for Princess Bari's gacha (leftovers turn into Crystals).

- Moon Rabbit Cookie pick-up event.

- CRASH! Rumble Arena event.

- Chuseok 7-day check-in, and a new 7-day check-in with Vampire Cookie.

### Not in the patch notes

- ▲ BuffNow gives Water allies +18% Max HP. It had no effect at all before this update.

- ChangeGuided souls lose 30% damage on each repeat hit against the same enemy (100 / 70 / 49%), so her total against a single boss is lower than the raw number suggests.

Improvements and fixes (7)

- Broken plates can be repaired with Metal Syrup, and a break now drops the plate only 2 levels.

- Hold the level-up button to pick the target level.

- Oven: change presets with drawn gear; filter up to 5 sub-stats in auto bake.

- Chat translation button.

- CookieTalk and Adventure Note updates.

- Guild Conquest: the group with fewer than 5 guilds is now the last group instead of a top one.

- Quest 34731 rewards order fixed (21 more Rune Crystals, 18,000 fewer Coins in total).

## Cherry Cola Cookie

Cherry Cola

SSR · Water · Charge

Tart Cherry Slash: charges at high-ATK enemies and damages the enemies around her.

Every 1.75 s: 2 slashes of 182% ATK. Every 9 hits a dash finisher of 245% ATK.

### Expansions

- Main stages up to 328-30 (80 new chapters, new stage bosses).

- Daily Dungeons up to stage 570.

- Quest rewards for the new stages, including 1-hour Auto Hunt rewards.

- Gnome Laboratory up to 90-2 (special research 16), Fame up to level 360, Resolve up to stage 1,500.

### Balance

- When buffs or debuffs conflict, the one that applies is now decided by its value after all stats are applied, Skill AMP included.

### Events

- Cherry Cola Cookie pick-up event.

- Golden Brown Lucky Dough Bake.

- 7-day check-in with Melon Soda Cookie.

Improvements and fixes (15)

- Boss HP bars show decimals (no more 0% while the boss is still alive).

- Chat: turn off the preview, no jump to the bottom while scrolling, recent messages kept, report messages and nicknames.

- CookieTalk on/off switch.

- Auto Arena until tickets run out.

- Resolve: the x100 button is now MAX.

- Plating toggles keep their setting.

- No gacha animation for cookies and pets you already own (TSSR still shows).

- Event missions count progress made before the update.

- Your place is kept in the Defense Record after a battle.

- Sugar Rune crafting: system messages no longer block the animation.

- The Mission pop-up opens on a tab that has rewards ready.

- The Sugarcoating boost also applies when full Auto Hunt rewards are auto-collected.

- Cookies could be controlled by hand in Crumble Dungeon and Guild Conquest.

- Lag in the Organize Gear window.

- Misplaced "EXP Dungeon Level" / "Coin Dungeon Level" text in the Adventure Record in some languages.

Brightseeker

Pinot Noir

SSR · Dark · Support

Shadow Bind: gives allies Lift RES and Rapid Fire synergy; her shadow bullets cut enemy healing.

Every 9.5 s: all allies Lift RES +35% for 10 s. Shadow bullets 90.4% ATK, 80% chance to cut enemy healing by 70% for 5 s.

Tea Knight

SSR · Light · Tank

From the Guild Medal Exchange. Gives allies Boss DMG in Guild Conquest.

Every 10 s: thunder strike of 155.2% ATK and all allies Boss DMG +70% for 10 s (Guild Conquest only; +45% at the lowest grade).

SSR · Pet

## Cool Mint Cookie and the Rune Crystal Dungeon

Cool Mint

SSR · Water · Charge

Implant Excavation: drills into the ground, knocking enemies away, and summons skulls from the dug-up ground.

### Cookie & pet changes

- ▲ BuffSkill AMP now boosts their ATK buff and shields.

- ChangeSummon ATK now shown with the multiplier applied (26.7% → 74.8% at 9★). Display only, real damage unchanged.

### New

- Rune Crystal Dungeon: beat the White Sugar Guardian for Rune Crystals (used to craft Sugar Runes).

### Balance

- Resolve after level 101, ATK and DEF per level:+1 → +2

- Resolve after level 101, HP per level:+10 → +20

- Skill AMP now raises summons' ATK, DEF and HP.

### Events

- King Choco Drop's Roulette.

- 7-day check-in with Pomegranate Cookie.

- Rune Crystal Dungeon Conquest event.
