Frequently Asked Questions

What is CookieRun: Crumble?

A deck-building idle survivor RPG from Devsisters: a team of up to 12 Cookies fights enemy swarms automatically while you focus on team building, the three classes and pet pairings.

When did CookieRun: Crumble release?

It launched globally on July 30, 2026 for iOS, Android and PC via Google Play Games, with 69 Cookies and 54 Pets at launch. The August 27 update added Brightseeker, Pinot Noir and Tea Knight plus the Battery Mellow Pet; this site labels launch and post-launch counts separately. Pre-registration has closed.

Is CookieRun: Crumble free to play?

Yes — free to download and play, with optional in-app purchases.

Do pre-registration rewards carry over into the game?

They were. Sign-up and milestone rewards such as Crystals, Premium Cookie Cutters and Premium Pet Tickets were delivered in-game at launch, and the mailbox window closed on August 29, 2026 — anything left unclaimed then is gone.
