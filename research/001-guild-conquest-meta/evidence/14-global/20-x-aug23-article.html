[Patch] About the August 23 Update

CookieRun: Crumble@CRCrumbleEN·Aug 26

Greetings from the CookieRun: Crumble team!

We’re excited to let all of our Mercenaries know what’s new, improved, and in store for the update on August 27 (GMT+9).

Details regarding the update are outlined below. We’ll keep doing our best to make sure everyone can enjoy their adventures through the Crumble world.

📌 KEY UPDATES

■ NEW COOKIE – (TSSR) BRIGHTSEEKER COOKIE
Welcome this bright, engineer with no fear of failure to your Mercenary crew!

- Brightseeker Cookie is a Light Ranged Cookie who is prepared to unleash her “Satellite Shock” upon your enemies!

- Satellite Shock summons her Lollipop Drones to dish out powerful laser attacks and shockwaves, dealing significant damage.

■ NEW COOKIE – (SSR) PINOT NOIR COOKIE
The well-aged priest of the D’vine Abbey is prepared to bless your Mercenaries.

- Pinot Noir Cookie is an SSR Dark Support Cookie whose “Shadow Bind” will provide a bit of extra support for your Mercenaries in a pinch.

- Shadow Bind helps keep your Mercenaries grounded as it grants Lift RES and Rapid Fire Synergy, while the Cookie’s shadow bullets also reduce your enemy’s incoming healing.

■ NEW PET – (SSR) CHARGEMELLOW
Everyone could use an energizer Pet, and Chargemellow is one for sure!

- Chargemellow grants Rapid Fire Synergy to your allies.

■ NEW GAME CONTENT – GUILD CONQUEST
Join forces with your Guildmates to take on the Extra-Stuffed Piñata in this cooperative, competitive mode!

- The total damage dealt by your Guildmates is converted into your Guild’s score and will be ranked and rewarded on a weekly basis.
- Each Season, Guilds will be matched into Groups of 5 Guilds. Rank based on your Guild’s score within your Group, and your Guild’s score against all guilds!
- Use the Guild Medals earned through Guild Conquest in the Guild Medal Exchange to stock up on important items and resources!

■ GUILD MEDAL EXCHANGE EXCLUSIVE COOKIE – (SSR) TEA KNIGHT COOKIE
Invite Tea Knight Cookie to join your Mercenary crew through the Guild Medal Exchange!

- Tea Knight Cookie is an SSR Light Tank Cookie that grants allies a Boss DMG buff in Guild Conquest, making him extra effective when taking on that nasty Piñata!

■ MAIN STAGE & RESOLVE EXPANSION
- Main Stages are expanded to Stage 248-30.
- Resolve can be leveled up to Resolve Level 1000, allowing our most dedicated Mercenaries to make their teams that much stronger.

■ CookieRun: Crumble x 60chicken “CCC Chicken” Collab
We’ve C-C-C-Crumbled our way all the way to a c-c-c-collab with popular Korean chicken brand 60chicken’s tasty treat—CCC Chicken! Defeat the CCC Chickenbot during the event to get your dough on extra crispy rewards!

- Use CCC Chickenbot Tickets to summon the three different flavors of CCC Chickenbot enemies.
- Earn the Tickets from the Chicken Out? CCC Chickenbot event missions.
- Use the event currency earned by defeating the Chickenbots in the Event Exchange to collect all sorts of items!

■ COOKIETALK & ADVENTURE NOTE UPDATES
- Check out the Cookies’ backstories and conversations with the new CookieTalk and Adventure Note updates!

- Craft Sugar Runes to make your Mercenaries even stronger and collect even more Rune Crystals along with other important resources!

■ NEW CHECK-IN EVENTS (7-DAY / 25-DAY) EVENTS

■ NEW COOKIE RELEASE EVENT

- Light of Knowledge Event: Collect the “Light of Knowledge,” the resource needed to pull in the Gacha for TSSR Brightseeker Cookie.
- Any Lights of Knowledge remaining after Brightseeker Cookie’s Special Gacha ends will be converted to Crystals and delivered to your mailbox.

- Pinot Noir Cookie Pick-up Event: Complete missions to get resources to help you get Pinot Noir Cookie from the Pick-up Gacha!
- Unused Pick-up Points are reset when the event ends.

■ SUGAR RUNE ARTISAN EVENT

- Craft Sugar Runes to make your Mercenaries even stronger and collect even more Rune Crystals along with other important resources!

■ NEW CHECK-IN EVENTS (7-DAY / 25-DAY)

- Just check-in every day and get your dough on all sorts of key resources to build your Mercenary crew!

🛠️ IMPROVEMENTS

■ GIVE UP/RETRY BOSS FIGHTS
- Now you can immediately choose to give up or retry a Boss battle during the Main Stages!

■ CHANGES TO MANUAL COOKIE CONTROL
- When manually controlling your Mercenaries, now tapping and dragging will move not just the closest Cookies but your entire Mercenary crew!

■ IMPROVEMENTS FOR BOSS FIGHT CONTROL
- CookieTalks no longer appear during active Boss fights, so now you no longer have to worry about accidentally tapping on an overly talkative Cookie! (C’mon guys, focus on the fight!)
- Tapping and dragging over the Quest, Profile, and other buttons no longer blocks or hinders manual Cookie control.

■ ADJUSTMENT TO AUTO BAKE COUNTS
- The maximum number of Gear that can be Auto Baked has been raised from 20 to 50.

■ GUILD QoL IMPROVEMENTS
- A pop-up will appear when attempting to donate over a certain number of Crystals to the Guild Research Center.
- The Guild Help Center window no longer closes after tapping Help All.

■ IMPROVEMENTS TO ARENA TIERS
- The Arena Tiers will be reformatted in order to expand the highest ranking Tiers and ensure more accurate matching for ranks.
- Champion 1, Champion 2, and Champion 3 will be added as Tiers for the top three ranked players.
- Master 4 – Grand Master 1 Teirs will have their requirements changed to be based on set Ranks as opposed to subjective positioning (Top 10%, etc.)

🐞 BUG FIXES AND OTHER INFORMATION

■ BUG FIXES
- The Dough Dungeon no longer occasionally shows the Boss’s total HP as -1%.
- The Arena Guide now covers Pet Collection Effects.
- Cookies no longer wander outside of the intended scanning area when granting Healing or Buffs to allies.
- Error causing the incorrect value to be applied for overlapping buffs/debuffs has been fixed. (The strongest/highest value effect is applied when multiple buffs or debuffs overlap or conflict.)
- The Gnome Laboratory now displays the red update dot as soon as Research is completed with Guild Help, as opposed to the time implied by its original countdown.
- Damage and Healing text displays now show as intended when the settings are ON.
- The shop now accurately displays when the Weekly Free Sugarcoating Membership is available for subscribing.
- Defense Teams in the Arena are now positioned as shown in their Lineups, as opposed to occasionally being positioned at random.

■ OTHER INFORMATION
- Adventure Records now show your Rune Crystal Dungeon Level.
- The max. number of certain items in the Victory Medal Exchange and the Mileage Exchange has been increased.
