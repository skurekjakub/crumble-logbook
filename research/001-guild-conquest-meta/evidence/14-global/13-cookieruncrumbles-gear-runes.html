CookieRun: Crumble Gear Guide: Stop Letting Bad Stats Fake Your Power

A bigger power number can still make your team worse. Learn which Oven gear and Sugar Rune stats deserve a lock, and which rolls only look expensive.

A green power arrow is hard to ignore. Equip the new piece, watch the number rise, and the decision seems finished. Then the boss lives longer or Scorpion Cookie misses the Venom Sting holding the rotation together.

Combat power matters because stages can punish an underpowered team. It still cannot tell you whether a stat helps that Cookie. Accuracy and Evasion can inflate the number. A support can gain power while losing Skill Haste.

Use power to enter the fight. Use the fight to judge the build.

Check why the team failed first

Run the stage once before touching gear. Watch what breaks.

If the timer expires while everyone is healthy, look for damage, faster skills, a missing debuff, or a power penalty. If the front line disappears before the first support skill, more CRIT DMG on the back line will not save it. That team needs Damage Reduction or healing.

Boss attempts expose consistency problems. A Scorpion build with Focus may clear more often because Venom Sting lands reliably. Full offense can clear faster when every application connects, but it may demand several restarts. I use Focus for farming and get greedy when I only need one win.

Change one thing and repeat the same stage. Replacing gear, Pets, and several Cookies together tells you nothing about which change worked.

Know what the useful stats do

ATK raises damage and can improve healing when a skill scales with it. DEF reduces incoming damage. HP gives a larger health pool, while Damage Reduction cuts damage directly. If a tank gets erased before help arrives, I test Damage Reduction first.

CRIT Chance controls how often critical hits occur. Going above 100% can still pay because the bonus can apply more than once. CRIT DMG raises the payoff. CRIT Resistance matters more in PvP, where surviving the opener can decide the match.

Accuracy fights Evasion. Both can help in a specific matchup, but neither is a safe default for story gear. Do not keep them just because the power total approves.

Focus helps debuffs land through Resistance. It matters when poison or DEF Down controls the damage window. Skill Amp improves skill output. Skill Haste shortens cooldowns. Both fit almost every role and move easily between Cookies.

Boss Damage and Elemental Damage can beat general stats in the right fight. Move Speed can also solve a positioning problem. Keep these as matchup pieces, not automatic upgrades.

Respect power breakpoints without chasing every arrow

Story stages can reduce your damage when the team sits below the expected power. One late-story example shows how severe that can get: about 6 million power on Chapter 42-1 dealt roughly 55% damage, 10 million restored normal damage, and 12 million reached around 120%. Those numbers explain the system. They are not universal targets.

A mediocre high-power piece may beat a clean low-power piece below the breakpoint. Once the team reaches normal damage, another Accuracy roll may add less to the clear than Skill Haste, Skill Amp, ATK, or CRIT.

The keep-or-sell check

Ask two questions: does the piece cross a useful power breakpoint, and does it improve the Cookie's actual job? If both answers are no, the green arrow is decoration.

Judge Oven gear in 20 seconds

Once your Oven produces Special gear regularly, treat Special or higher as the keep floor. Two substat slots support a real build. Lower gear can fill an empty slot, but it should not eat many materials.

Check a drop in this order:

  1. Will it cross the current stage power breakpoint?
  2. Do both substats help the Cookie's job?
  3. Could Skill Amp or Skill Haste make it useful on another Cookie later?
  4. Does it carry a strong role stat, such as ATK or CRIT for DPS, Damage Reduction for a tank, or Focus for a debuffer?

Accuracy, Evasion, Resistance, and Move Speed are easy to overvalue. Focus does nothing without a useful debuff, yet it can rescue a boss rotation that falls apart after one miss.

Build the team you use instead of chasing twelve perfect sets. I would keep a lower-power Skill Haste piece before an Accuracy upgrade with no clear owner. The Oven will make another shiny mistake soon enough.

Build Sugar Runes around each role

Supports have the cleanest targets. Start with Skill Amp and Skill Haste, then consider ATK if healing scales with it. Macaron Cookie and Pomegranate Cookie appear in enough teams to justify early investment. A good Rune on a regular support beats a perfect Rune on a specialist who rarely leaves the bench.

DPS Cookies can use Skill Amp, Skill Haste, ATK, CRIT Chance, and CRIT DMG. Aggressive locking gets expensive. I leave the exact-stat filter open early, accept a strong offensive result, and lock only after the Rune gives me something worth protecting. Vampire Cookie has no reason to force Focus when plain damage fits the job.

Tanks still like Skill Amp and Skill Haste. Pick Damage Reduction when the front line dies early. Madeleine Cookie and Nameless Cake Hound can use CRIT when they already survive and the timer is the problem.

PvP raises the value of Damage Reduction and CRIT Resistance. Boss teams care more about Boss Damage, Focus, and reliable timing. There is no single perfect Rune that ignores the mode and the Cookie's kit.

Use Focus when missed debuffs cost the run

Scorpion Cookie and Devil Cookie are the clearest Focus users. Scorpion wants Venom Sting to stack quickly. Devil Cookie wants DEF Down active before the damage window. One miss can make a strong team look broken.

Focus improves consistency, but it is not mandatory. An offensive Scorpion can win with a lucky sequence. Choose Focus for farming or a rotation that depends on the debuff. Choose offense for a one-clear push when you will retry.

Do not invent a magic Focus threshold. Boss Resistance values are not clear enough for that. Compare several attempts with each setup and keep the one that clears the way you need.

Spend Plating and rerolls where they matter

Plating affects every Cookie, so the Skill Amp book is my first choice. The ATK weapon and CRIT staff make sense next when timers block progress. One useful account-wide bonus changes more fights than tiny upgrades across every icon.

Sugar Rune locks become expensive fast. Early on, an R or better effect with the right stat is usable. Move toward SR and SSR as supplies improve. Keep an SSR result only when the stat fits, the roll is strong, and the Cookie stays in your active teams. Gold letters do not make a bad stat useful.

Set a budget before crafting. Pick one main-team Cookie, define a winning roll, and stop when the budget is gone. Support Runes justify earlier locks because their target pool is narrow. Forcing three exact DPS lines usually wastes materials.

Promotion-gated slots take longer to fill. Protect a rare useful line, but do not lock a poor result because it took weeks to reach the slot. Sunk cost has eaten enough accounts.

Use this quick build sheet

  • General gear: reach the current power breakpoint, then favor Skill Amp and Skill Haste.
  • Supports: Skill Amp, Skill Haste, and ATK when support output scales with it.
  • DPS: Skill Amp, Skill Haste, ATK, CRIT Chance, and CRIT DMG.
  • Tanks: add Damage Reduction for survival or CRIT when a damage-capable tank already lives.
  • Debuffers: add Focus when consistency beats restarting.
  • PvP: give defensive and matchup stats more weight.

Check each Cookie's Role and buffs in the Cookie list, compare working formations on the Teams page, or test your roster in the Team Builder. The tier list can guide rare investments, but a lower-ranked Cookie used in five modes may still deserve materials first.

CookieRun: Crumble gear and Sugar Rune FAQ

What are the best general gear stats?

Skill Amp and Skill Haste are the safest general choices because every role relies on skills. Add ATK and CRIT for damage, Damage Reduction for fragile tanks, and Focus when the team depends on a debuff.

Should I always equip the item with higher combat power?

Use higher power when it crosses a stage damage breakpoint. Once the penalty is gone, judge the substats and the actual clear. Accuracy or Evasion can raise power without fixing damage, survival, or debuff consistency.

What should I lock on support Sugar Runes?

Lock strong Skill Amp and Skill Haste results on supports used across several modes. Add ATK when their healing or support effect scales with it. Stop when the next lock would delay upgrades for the rest of the main team.

Does Scorpion Cookie need Focus?

Focus makes Venom Sting more reliable against Resistance. A full offense build can still clear by retrying until the applications land. Pick Focus for farming and offense for a one-clear push.

When is an SSR Sugar Rune worth keeping?

Keep it when the stat fits the Cookie, the roll is strong enough to matter, and the Cookie stays in your working teams. A useful SR beats an SSR line that does nothing for the build.
