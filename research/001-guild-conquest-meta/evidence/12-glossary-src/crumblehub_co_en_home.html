A 100% value uses 375 Crystals = ₩1,500 (0.25 Crystals per ₩1) as the baseline. Only selected currencies affect conversion and ranking. Lifetime Membership rewards are calculated for the entered usage period. SR Cookies are excluded, and one TSSR Cookie is valued at 18,000 Light of Knowledge. Flames of Bravery are excluded from the public efficiency calculation because they are no longer sold. Chocosteel uses a 200💎 value based on the Cookie Cutter opportunity cost in Arena and event shops, while Syrup Metal uses 500💎 based on the Arena shop exchange rate. Guild Medals use a 1.25💎 value based on the Guild Shop exchange rates for Rune Crystals and Stellar Points. Speed-ups, offline rewards, and equipment are shown in package contents but excluded from totals because no conversion rate is currently set.
